<QuickAnswer>
In the full game, put **wood in the stove**, add **oats and milk** to the pot, then light the fire. The demo adds **sugar**. Berries come later, after Aldous says the served porridge is missing something.
</QuickAnswer>

## Find the ingredients

Take wood and oats from the servant's room. Milk sits in a crate near the maid. The pot and a dead mouse are in the upstairs service area.

You still need fire. Give the dead mouse to the cat in the portrait hall; it leaves the matches behind. Return to the servant's stove, add the wood, place the pot, pour in oats and milk, and ignite the wood.

## Add the berries

Serve Aldous the bowl, porridge, and spoon. When he complains that something is missing, open the bedroom window. The crow accepts the item from the shaving accident and leaves a key. Use it on the top drawer to get the berries.

<Spoiler>
After adding berries, a bug moves between open holes in the bowl. Drag berries to cover its possible exits. Keep reducing its available route until it runs into a closed position, then squash it. You do not need to restart the whole breakfast if it changes direction.
</Spoiler>

Finally, carry the spoon to Aldous's mouth until he has eaten enough.

<Note>
If your written recipe mentions sugar, you are playing the demo build. The launch walkthrough's breakfast sequence uses oats and milk before the berry stage.
</Note>
