<QuickAnswer>
Servant of the Lake is a **prequel to Rusty Lake: Roots**. You work a weekend at the Vanderboom estate while Aldous, William, and Alexandra sit at the center of a new family mystery.
</QuickAnswer>

## The setup

You arrive as a newly hired servant. Ordinary duties—breakfast, cleaning, receiving guests—become the interface for exploring the Vanderboom house and its alchemical secrets. The official description presents the game as a single-player point-and-click mystery.

## The three Vanderbooms

Official development notes identify **Aldous, William, and Alexandra** as the story's three central figures. Their painted portraits are more than decoration: the game repeatedly turns observation of each sibling into puzzle information.

## Where it belongs in the series

Rusty Lake says the project began by revisiting the house and background of *Rusty Lake: Roots*, and calls the finished game a prequel to that story. You can therefore play it first chronologically. Long-time players will recognize names, places, and visual motifs that carry extra meaning.

You do **not** need to finish every Cube Escape title before starting. For the strongest immediate context, play *Rusty Lake: Roots* either before this game for recognition or after it to follow the chronology forward.

## The art is part of the clue language

The estate landscapes use layered physical paintings to produce parallax, while the three sibling portraits were painted on canvas. That matters in play: animation and small changes inside paintings often communicate numbers, directions, or sequence.
