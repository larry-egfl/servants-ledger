<QuickAnswer>
Open the curtains → set the clock to **7:00** → put on both slippers → shave and clean Aldous → serve porridge → exercise **L, R, D, R, R, D** → dress him.
</QuickAnswer>

## Collect everything before waking him

Search both sides of the bedroom for a bowl, spoon, two slippers, cloth, and clock hand. Solve the floor box by placing each patterned tile over its matching sample; it contains the razor. Move the hanging clothes to expose the cloth.

## Follow the note literally

The bedside routine is also the solution order. Open the bed curtains first. Put the recovered hand on the alarm clock and set the small hand to VII and the large hand to XII. Aldous then wakes at 7:00.

Do not skip the note about cold feet: place **both slippers** before using the razor. Clean the resulting cut with the cloth.

## Breakfast and exercise

Hand Aldous the bowl, add the cooked porridge, then give him the spoon. The berries and bug sequence must be finished before he will eat. See the [porridge puzzle guide](/en/guides/porridge-puzzle) if that stage is still blocked.

The small soldier on the dresser demonstrates the exercise pattern. Repeat:

**Left → Right → Down → Right → Right → Down**

Then drag the clothing pieces onto Aldous. Once fully dressed, speak to him and inspect the window to move the day forward.
