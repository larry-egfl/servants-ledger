<QuickAnswer>
Click animated details twice, write down numbers **with the character or object that produced them**, and read the Servant's Notebook after every completed duty.
</QuickAnswer>

## Before asking for a code

1. **Read the order on the lock.** Finding three numbers is only half a solution; portraits or icons on the box usually tell you their order.
2. **Watch the whole animation.** Count blinks, fingers, watch shakes, or directional gestures.
3. **Record labels with values.** “Alexandra = 2” survives a room change better than a bare “2.”
4. **Check your version.** Demo codes and recipes can differ from the August 2026 full release.

## While exploring

5. **Collect before combining.** Sweep each room edge before using a new item; small tools often unlock a clue several rooms away.
6. **Revisit animals.** A cat, crow, horse, or goat may exchange an item rather than simply decorate the scene.
7. **Treat household notes as algorithms.** Their order is often mandatory, not flavor text.
8. **Use the notebook.** It lists duties, stored documents, your notes, and missing achievement slots.

## When stuck

9. **Reset only the small puzzle first.** Sliding or movement puzzles normally allow a local retry; do not restart the chapter immediately.
10. **Reveal the smallest hint.** Start with the room or governing rule, then use the numeric answer only if needed. It keeps the game's discovery intact.

Because chapters can be replayed, your first run does not need to collect every hidden achievement. Finish the story at your own pace and clean up missables afterward.
