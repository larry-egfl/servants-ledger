<QuickAnswer>
The demo's portrait box code is **235**, its porridge uses **milk + oats + sugar**, the clock is set to **7:00**, and Aldous exercises **Left, Right, Down, Right, Right, Down**.
</QuickAnswer>

## Get into the house

Open the carriage curtains. Take the carrot and the two loose tin cups—one at a window, one beneath the coach. Put them beside the other cups and sort all five from one stripe through five stripes. Take the coin, pay the coachman, and collect the iron bar after he leaves.

Pry up the board near the planter. The key underneath opens the estate. Take the crow feather, ink it at the table, and sign your contract.

## Make the porridge

Clicking the three portraits reveals the demo code. Alexandra shows **2**, Aldous signals **3**, and William blinks **5**, so enter **235** on the upstairs box to obtain sugar.

Collect milk, oats, the pot, wood, and matches. The matches are under the cat; give it the dead mouse from the wall opening so it moves. At the stove:

1. Insert wood and light it.
2. Put the pot on top.
3. Add milk and oats.
4. Add sugar, then collect the porridge.

## Finish Aldous's routine

In the bedroom, collect both slippers, the bowl, spoon, razor, cloth, and clock hand. Match the patterned tiles on the small box to take the razor. Open the bed curtain and set the clock to **VII / XII**, or 7:00.

<Spoiler>
Put on both slippers, shave Aldous, and clean the cut. Give him the bowl, spoon, and porridge. Trade the severed earlobe to the bird for a drawer key, then add the drawer's berries to the bowl. Move berries to close the holes the bug approaches; squash it once trapped.
</Spoiler>

Feed him until he refuses more food. Copy the soldier's movement pattern—**L, R, D, R, R, D**—then dress him in the requested order. Look through the window and tap the approaching carriage to end the demo.

<Note>
The demo and full game are separate builds. Do not use **235** on the same full-release box; that version uses **243**.
</Note>
