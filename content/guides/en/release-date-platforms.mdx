**Servant of the Lake launched August 13, 2026.** Rusty Lake links the game on Steam, itch.io, Apple's App Store, and Google Play.
</QuickAnswer>

## Where to play

- **PC / Mac:** Steam and itch.io.
- **iPhone / iPad:** App Store.
- **Android:** Google Play.

Rusty Lake describes it as a premium, single-player point-and-click adventure. It is not a free browser episode. A separate free demo remains useful if you want to check the tone and controls before buying, but its puzzle details are not always identical to the full release.

## Is it part of Rusty Lake?

Yes. The developer and publisher are Rusty Lake, and the game returns to the Vanderboom estate. The official store description connects it to the creators of Cube Escape, The Past Within, and Underground Blossom.

## Launch discount

Steam currently shows a **25% launch discount**. Exact prices and the end time can vary by region, so use the live store rather than a copied dollar figure.
