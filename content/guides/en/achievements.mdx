<QuickAnswer>
You can replay chapters after finishing, and the **Servant's Notebook shows missing achievements**. The safest first missable is at arrival: pet the horse repeatedly before progressing.
</QuickAnswer>

## Do you need a perfect first run?

No. Chapter replay becomes available, so a missed hidden action does not require deleting your whole save. Use the notebook to see which duty still hides an achievement, then revisit that chapter.

## Verified hidden actions

These launch-day actions have direct playthrough or player-report support:

- **The Arrival:** pet the horse repeatedly before completing its normal task.
- **Preparing the Reception:** inspect and clean the small painting in the servant's room; the extra damage creates a later repair opportunity.
- **Welcoming the Guests:** offer the bouquet to the goat before milking it.
- **The Night Watch:** keep checking the garden window until the masked figure appears.
- **Searching the Attic:** interact with the crow while working on the constellation sequence.
- **Going Hunting:** the “perfect” route involves treating William as a target before finishing the animals.
- **Doing the Washing:** inspect the bathroom through the keyhole.
- **Night Time Duties:** use the balcony as a disposal route before descending to the cellar.

<Note>
This is intentionally not labeled a complete list. The source achievement log is still being updated on launch day, so empty duty headings are not converted into guesses here.
</Note>
