<QuickAnswer>
Collect **three candles**, place them in the chandelier, and light the full layout as **one continuous winding arrow**. The locked candle box uses **243** in the full release.
</QuickAnswer>

## Find all three candles

One candle is on the servant's-room table. The maid hands you another after the portrait-dusting task. The final candle is locked upstairs.

For that box, dust the portraits and count each response. Read the faces shown on the box in their printed order: Alexandra 2, Aldous 4, William 3. Enter **243** and take the candle.

## Read the chandelier as a path

Climb to the entrance chandelier and fill its three empty holders. The puzzle is not asking you to sort by candle height or light the outside first. Treat the arrangement as a connect-the-dots diagram: every next flame should continue the direction of a single curling arrow through the whole layout.

If the pattern breaks, stop instead of clicking randomly. Reset the chandelier, identify the arrow's tail at the edge, and follow each nearest candle along the bend toward its pointed end. The successful order uses every candle once.
