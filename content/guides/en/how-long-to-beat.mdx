<QuickAnswer>
The **demo officially takes about 15–30 minutes**. Rusty Lake has not published an official full-game hour count, and launch-day completion data is not yet stable.
</QuickAnswer>

## Demo length

The free demo covers the arrival and Aldous's morning routine. Its Steam page gives an expected play time of 15 to 30 minutes. A first blind run may take longer if you avoid hints.

## Full game length

The full release spans **three in-game days** with multiple duties per day, hidden achievements, documents, and replayable chapters. That makes it substantially larger than the demo, but it does not justify inventing a precise hour estimate on launch day.

Your actual time will depend on three things:

- whether you have played other Rusty Lake puzzle games;
- how often you use the Servant's Notebook or outside hints;
- whether you chase hidden achievements on the first run.

We will add a median only after enough completed runs exist to support it.
