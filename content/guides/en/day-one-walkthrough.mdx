<QuickAnswer>
Day One begins with the **cup order 1–5** outside the estate and later uses **243** for the full-release portrait box. Aldous wakes at **7:00**, and his exercise order is **Left, Right, Down, Right, Right, Down**.
</QuickAnswer>

## Arrival at the estate

Open the carriage curtains and collect the tin cup. Outside, take the carrot and find the second cup at the left window. At the right window, place both cups with the three already there and arrange all five by stripe count, lowest to highest. The opened cup gives you the coachman's coin.

Pay him, feed the horse, and keep talking until he leaves. Take the iron bar, pry up the loose board beside the planter, and use the hidden key on the front door.

Inside, take the feather from the maid, dip it in the desk ink, and sign the contract. Speak to her again to receive the **Servant's Notebook**. It tracks duties, documents, notes, and missed achievements—check it whenever the next objective feels unclear.

## Prepare Aldous's breakfast

The servant's room contains oats, firewood, and the porridge instructions. Put wood in the stove. Continue through the house, take milk from the crate, then collect the pot and dead mouse upstairs. Feed the mouse to the cat to recover the matches.

Return to the stove. Add the **oats and milk** to the pot, light the fire, and take the cooked porridge upstairs. The launch version does not use the demo's sugar step here.

## Wake and dress Aldous

In his room, collect the bowl, one slipper, cloth, second slipper, spoon, and clock hand. The patterned floor box opens when each movable tile matches the sample below it; take the razor.

<Spoiler>
Open the bed curtain, attach the clock hand, and set the clock to **7:00**. Put both slippers on Aldous before shaving him. After the shaving accident, clean him with the cloth. Give him the bowl, porridge, and spoon. Feed the severed piece to the crow outside the window, then use its key on the top drawer for the berries.
</Spoiler>

Once the berries enter the bowl, block the bug's available holes until it can be squashed. Feed Aldous, then repeat the toy soldier's sequence: **Left → Right → Down → Right → Right → Down**. Dress him and inspect the arriving carriage.

## Prepare the reception

Clean every dirty patch, collect the candles, and dust all three Vanderboom portraits. Their full-release signals are William **3**, Alexandra **2**, and Aldous **4**. Read the character order printed on the locked box: Alexandra, Aldous, William. That makes the code **243**.

Place the three recovered candles in the chandelier. The lighting order must draw one unbroken winding arrow across the candle layout. Our dedicated [chandelier puzzle guide](/en/guides/chandelier-puzzle) explains the rule without story spoilers.
